Good to know

Booking deposit

A 20% deposit (minimum $50) secures your booking and comes off your final bill. It's non-refundable if you cancel or reschedule with less than 24 hours' notice.

Pet safety

Please remove all pets from the residence area and secure them in a locked place, such as a closed room, crate or secure yard, before your clean starts.

Satisfaction guarantee

Let us know within 24 hours if something was missed and we'll come back to fix it at no charge. End-of-lease cleans are covered for 72 hours.

No GST

Samira Home Decor is not registered for GST, so no GST is added to your price. You'll receive an invoice for every clean.
