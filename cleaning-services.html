Safety, access & liability

For your peace of mind, your Samira Cleaning Services cleaner is police-checked. Please read and agree to the terms below. You also give permission for the property to be accessed and cleaned at the agreed times, with or without you present.

  1. Access and keys. Keys must be supplied by the owner, the real estate agent or a lockbox at the property. Samira Cleaning Services does not accept keys sent to a private home address. How the key will be collected and returned must be agreed in writing before the booking is confirmed. Once the key has been returned as agreed, Samira Cleaning Services is no longer responsible for it. If access to the property cannot be gained at the booked time, a lockout fee may apply and the deposit may be kept.
  2. Payment. A 20% deposit of the total quote is required to confirm the booking; please allow 2 business days for the payment to clear. The remaining balance must be paid in full before keys are returned or the property is handed back. We do not accept cheques, overpayments or requests to pay third parties (such as couriers or movers). Fraudulent or reversed payments: if any payment is found to be fraudulent, unauthorised, made with stolen card or account details, or is later reversed or charged back, the client is fully responsible for the total amount owed, plus any bank fees, chargeback fees and recovery costs. Samira Cleaning Services may cancel or stop the booking at any time if a payment is suspected to be fraudulent, and any suspected fraud will be reported to the bank, the payment provider and the police.
  3. Utilities and property readiness. Water and electricity must be connected and working on the day of the clean. The property should be clear of personal items and furniture unless agreed otherwise, so all areas can be reached. Where the property has no parking access (for example, in the Adelaide CBD), a city parking fee will be added to the invoice.
  4. Safety. Any task considered unsafe may be declined, including work at heights beyond a standard step ladder, damaged or unstable fixtures, exposed wiring, or hazardous conditions. Light surface mould can be treated as part of a deep clean, but heavy, long-standing or embedded mould is not included and needs a mould specialist. Biohazards (such as bodily fluids, sharps or animal waste), pest infestations and asbestos are also not included and will not be cleaned. You must tell us about any known hazards at the property before the clean, including pets, security systems and structural issues.
  5. Property condition and damage. The property may be photographed before and after the clean to record its condition. Samira Cleaning Services is not responsible for pre-existing damage, general wear and tear, or loose, faulty or poorly fitted items (including blinds, fittings, fans and appliances). Some marks, stains or damage may not be fully removable; reasonable care is always used but complete removal cannot be guaranteed. Any damage caused during the clean must be reported within 24 hours of completion.
  6. Cancellations and changes. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the booked time may forfeit the deposit. Changes to the scope of work on the day may change the final price and will be agreed with you first.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you cover?
We clean homes and businesses across Adelaide and the surrounding suburbs. Not sure if we reach you? Request a call back or call 0451 609 398.
How do I book a clean?
Pick a date and time in the booking calendar, send your details, and complete the onboarding form. A 20% deposit confirms your booking and comes off your final bill.
Do I need to be home during the clean?
No. You can provide a key, lockbox code or arrange access through your agent, and give permission for the property to be cleaned with or without you present. Keys are handled securely and never labelled with the address.
Do you bring cleaning products and equipment?
You can provide your own products at no charge, or add Samira's cleaning agents for +$25. Either way is set when you book.
How much does a clean cost?
Standard cleans start from $230. Use the instant estimate to get a price for your clean type and home size. The estimate is a guide — your final price is confirmed when you book.
What is the deposit and cancellation policy?
A 20% deposit (minimum $50) secures your booking and comes off the final bill. Deposits are non-refundable for cancellations made with less than 48 hours' notice.
Is there a parking fee?
Only where the property has no parking access — for example in the Adelaide CBD — a $30 city parking fee is added to the invoice. We'll confirm if it applies when we book you in.
Is the cleaner police-checked?
Yes — your Samira Cleaning Services cleaner is police-checked, for your peace of mind.
Is there anything you don't clean?
We can treat light, surface mould as part of a deep clean, but we don't remove heavy, long-standing or embedded mould — that's a mould specialist's job. For safety we also don't handle biohazards (such as bodily fluids, sharps or animal waste), pest infestations or asbestos, and we may decline work at heights beyond a standard step ladder or in hazardous conditions.
What about my pets?
Please remove all pets from the residence area and secure them in a locked place — a closed room, crate or secure yard — before your clean starts.
