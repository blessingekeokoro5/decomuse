Why design is impactful to vacation rentals

You only have a split second to make a great first impression on a traveller, and nothing makes people scroll past faster than dull, unimpressive or forgettable décor. Investing in beautiful, considered design is the smartest thing a savvy rental owner can do, it wins the click, the booking and the five-star review.

Our Process
🎯

Scope

We meet to determine budget, timing, style and how guests will use the space, then decide what each room needs and plan the layout.

💲

Pricing

Using everything we've gathered, we send a proposal and a personalised quote tailored to your property, priced per project.

💡

Creative Tank

Our designers meet to determine the style and colour palette of the furnishings. A style board is created for your review and approval.

✅

Approval

Once you're happy with everything, you approve the plan and we confirm. A deposit secures your booking, and we begin making your space unforgettable.

🚚

Installation

We receive, inspect and assemble everything, then our team sets up the property, photo-ready and guest-ready. After that, all you do is list it and enjoy the bookings.
