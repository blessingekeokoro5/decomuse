Fees are as per the agreed quote. Prices are in AUD and include GST where applicable. Product purchases and third-party costs are additional unless stated.

3. Deposit & payment

A deposit of 50% is required to secure your booking and dates; the balance is due on or before completion / installation. Bookings are confirmed once the deposit is received.

4. Hire items (staging)

For staging, furniture & décor supplied on hire remain the property of DecoMuse and are provided for the agreed hire period. The client is responsible for reasonable care of hired items while on site.

5. Cancellations & rescheduling

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice to reschedule. Deposits are non-refundable within 7 days of a scheduled service, as time and inventory are reserved for you.

6. Access & site

The client will provide safe, timely access to the property and ensure the space is ready for styling on the agreed date.
