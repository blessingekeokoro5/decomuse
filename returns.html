Returns & Refunds

Lodge a return, get your reference barcode to attach to the parcel, track its progress, and choose a refund to your gift card or a replacement. Returns are sent at the customer's own cost.

📦 How it works: submit your details, reason and photos → we generate a reference barcode → print it and attach it to your parcel → post it back at your own cost → once we receive it, choose a refund to your gift card or a replacement.
