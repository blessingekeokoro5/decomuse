What we look for

Our supplier criteria

  • Quality, well-made products that last
  • A considered, on-brand aesthetic
  • Ethically & responsibly sourced where possible
  • Reliable stock & dispatch, or dropship capability
  • Fair wholesale / trade pricing
  • Australian-made or ready to ship to Australia

New and emerging makers are welcome, we love championing local talent.
