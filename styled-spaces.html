The method

How a new home gets styled

Three steps, no renovation required.

01

Walk the light

Samira looks at where you drop your keys, where you drink your tea and where the afternoon sun lands. The styling follows those habits.

02

Layer texture

Rug, lamps, linen, timber. Hard new-build surfaces stop echoing the moment the soft layers go in.

03

Edit, don't stuff

One hero piece. Three textures. Two heights on a surface. One living plant. Repeated in every room.

Room by room

Rooms we finish

Living · Bedroom · Kitchen · Hall

Living

A rug large enough for the sofa legs, two or three cushions, and room left to breathe.

Bedroom

A layered bed, paired lamps, one piece of art and something green on the dresser.

Kitchen

Three bench zones only. Everything else stays clear, so the kitchen still feels new.
