Resealable stand-up pouch, zipper top, food-grade

A premium, food-safe stand-up pouch that keeps your product fresh and looks beautiful on the shelf. The multi-layer PET + PA + PE structure locks in freshness and blocks moisture and odour, while the resealable zipper top lets customers open and close it again and again. Made with recycled materials and finished with vibrant flexo printing, it's an easy way to give your food brand a professional, retail-ready look.
