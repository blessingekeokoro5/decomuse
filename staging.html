Our process

How we bring your space to life

From first consultation to the final reveal, a considered, hands-on approach at every step.

🎯

Scope

We meet to understand your space, style, timing and budget, then measure up and map out what each room needs.

📋

Proposal

Using everything we've gathered, we send a tailored proposal and a personalised quote for your project.

🎨

Creative

Our stylists develop the style, colour palette and furnishings, and create a mood board for your review.

✅

Approval

Once you're happy with everything, you approve the plan and we confirm dates and begin sourcing your pieces.
